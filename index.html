What you'll walk out able to do

  1. Name the five parts of a professional portfolio: Home, About, Case Studies, Skills, Contact.
  2. Turn any project into a CAR case study (Challenge, Action, Result) with a number in the result.
  3. Write a two-sentence About Me that says who you are and the value you bring.
  4. Pick a platform that fits your field, and commit to it today.
  5. Apply show-don't-tell visual storytelling.
