Facilitator guide

Run of show, 45 minutes

This is a working session, not a lecture. Every block ends with students doing something on the handout. Put slides.html full screen on the projector and press P to open the presenter view on your laptop screen: notes, next slide, clock, run of show, and the student question board.

TimeBlockWhat you doAsk the room
12:30DoorsProjector on slide 1. Handout link and check-in QR on screen. Greet, ask each arriving student what project they have in mind."What's one thing you've built this semester?"
0:00Google yourselfSlide 2. Start the 60-second timer. Students search their name on phones. Tap the tally as hands go up."What's the first thing a recruiter sees?"
0:03Why portfolios winSlides 3–4. Roadmap in 30 seconds. 7.4-second figure (Ladders, 2018). Take two answers to the proof question."'Detail-oriented' is a claim. What's one thing you've made that proves it?"
0:10Brand + CARSlides 5–7. Two minutes of silent drafting (Step 2 on handout). Volunteer reads theirs; type it into the builder. Build one CAR out loud from a student's project. Click the tab for majors in the room."Call out a project. Let's build the CAR together." "Where's the number in your result?"
0:18Choose your platformSlide 8. Run the dropdown with two students' majors. Show two live student portfolios if Wi-Fi allows. Show of hands by platform; tap the tally. Public commitment matters."Who's team GitHub? Behance? Notion?"
0:26Blueprint + sourcesSlides 9–10. Five pages, three to six case studies. Name Elevate activities (Forage, hackathons, AWS) as portfolio evidence."Name one thing you made this semester that nobody outside the class has seen."
0:33Build one case studySlide 11. Start the 8-minute timer. Walk the room; push for a number in every Result. At 8:00 start the 3-minute swap."Partner: ask one question their case study doesn't answer."
0:41Checklist + check-inSlides 12–13. Read the checklist fast. Leave the QR up. Students download or email the worksheet and book a review."What's the first page you'll publish this week?"

If the room goes quiet

  • Pick a student's project yourself and build the CAR on screen; ask them only for the number.
  • Ask for a show of hands instead of an answer; tap the tally so the screen moves.
  • Use the discipline tabs: "Who's in business? Here's yours."

Bring

  • Laptop and adapter, this site loaded (works offline once opened), handout link on the board.
  • Printed handouts for students without a device (handout.html prints cleanly).
  • Two live student portfolio links to show.
  • Sign-in: the 312 QR on slide 13 records attendance for Elevate credit.

After

  • Record attendance from 312 and log the session for Elevate workshop credit.
  • Reply to any emailed worksheets within two business days with one revision note each.
  • Reset the tallies on the poll page before the next session.

Edit the site

All dates, links, and embeds live in assets/config.js. Slide text is in slides.html; each slide is one <section class="slide">. Speaker notes are the NOTES array at the bottom of that file.
