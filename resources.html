Elevate experiences

Elevate is Illinois Tech's commitment that every student has access to hands-on experiences and mentorship. An Elevate experience is a structured, real-world activity that counts toward that commitment: research projects, internships and co-ops, study away, competitions, short courses, and similar hands-on opportunities. Each one is portfolio evidence waiting to be written up as a case study.

Real portfolios, by college

See what good looks like

Illinois Tech organizes its programs into three colleges. Every example below is a live, public portfolio or gallery you can open right now. Notice what they share: a clear headline, a few case studies with visuals, and a number in every result.

Tech Futures

Engineering, computing, health, science, and emerging technologies

  • Brittany Chiang: software engineer; one-page site with an experience timeline and project cards that link to live code.
  • academicpages: the free GitHub Pages template behind thousands of research, science, and data portfolios; fork it and publish in an hour.
  • Tableau Public, Viz of the Day: data and analytics portfolios judged daily; each profile is a working portfolio.
  • GitHub Pages: your repositories plus a README per project are already a portfolio.

Design Futures

Architecture, design, human-centered innovation, and the built environment

  • Ava Brown and Kenzie Tokuchi: Illinois Tech B.Arch students with public Archinect portfolios.
  • Illinois Tech architecture portfolio guidelines: what the College of Architecture itself asks to see.
  • Art x Architecture Gallery: student and faculty work on campus.
  • Behance and 27 UX portfolio examples (uxfolio): image-first case studies for design and UX.

Professional Futures

Law, business, leadership, policy, and professional practice

  • Tableau Public: business, finance, and marketing analysts publish dashboards as case studies.
  • Clippings.me writing portfolio examples: writing, communications, and legal-adjacent work samples.
  • Authory politics and policy portfolios: published analysis, op-eds, and briefs collected in one place.
  • LinkedIn Featured section: for business and law, the front door; link each featured item to a case study page.

The CAR template (copy it)

Title: Challenge: the problem, constraint, or stakes. Action: what I did, tools, decisions, hours. Result: the outcome, with a number. Tools: Visual proof: screenshot / photo / chart / model / repo link

The five-page blueprint

HomeName, headline, one strong visual, one-line brand
