Running the room

Students see Home, Slides, Handout, Poll, Q&A, and Resources. Everything on this page is for you.

Before doors (12:30)

  1. Extend the display (not mirror). Projector: slides.html, press F for full screen.
  2. Laptop: press P in the deck to open the presenter view (notes, next slide, clock, question board).
  3. In Microsoft Forms, open Questions and Feedback → View responses in a tab next to the presenter view.
  4. Put the student link on the first slide or the board. Theme: T toggles light/dark; dark reads better on most projectors.

Live poll (slides 2 and 8)

In Forms, open Portfolio Workshop Live Poll → Present. It shows a QR code, the short link, and a live bar chart or treemap that redraws as students answer; the arrows step through the three questions. Flip the projector to that window for the poll moments, then back to the deck.

Student link: forms.cloud.microsoft/r/pfXcjYeuB3 (also on the Poll page with a QR).

Questions (any time, and the last slide)

Students submit through the Q&A page or the QR on the final slide. Read them in the Forms Responses tab; paste the ones you'll answer on screen into the presenter view's question board and use Project the board for the closing Q&A.

Student link: forms.cloud.microsoft/r/0mf1JThAqw. The same form has an optional rating and feedback field.

What students leave with

  • The handout (saved on their device; download, email, print buttons at the top).
  • The resources page: real portfolios by college, platforms, the CAR template, Elevate experiences.
  • Your booking link for a 30-minute review, on every page and the last slide.
  • 312 check-in for Elevate credit (QR on the last slide and the Home page).
